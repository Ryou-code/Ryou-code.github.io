About me

Hi, I’m Qihang. I’m a master’s student in Computer Science at New York University and a research assistant working with Ying Ding at UT Austin.

My research interests include LLM reasoning, controlled evaluation, interpretability, and faithfulness. I’m interested in understanding how language models arrive at their answers, designing controlled evaluations to examine their reasoning, and studying whether their explanations faithfully reflect the information and processes they actually use.
